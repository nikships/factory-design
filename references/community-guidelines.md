# Factory Guild community guidelines

Source: <https://factory.com/guild/guidelines>, pulled 2026-10-25. "17 rules,
4 criteria." These govern how Guild members behave in Guild spaces and when
representing Factory publicly. The brand chapter matters most when you produce
material that carries Factory's name or look. Program details, ranks, and
submission formats live at <https://factory.com/ambassador>.

## 01 You're part of the brand

Guild members and community contributors are not Factory employees. But when
you publicly build with Factory, talk about it, or wear the Guild badge, people
associate you with Factory. Posts, talks, and comments shape how Factory is
perceived. Be mindful of that.

## 02 Rules for community spaces (Slack, Discord, forums)

- **02.1** Use threads. Keep channels readable.
- **02.2** Stay on topic. Product feedback goes in feedback channels, not general chat.
- **02.3** Don't spam. No unsolicited self-promotion, affiliate links, or repeated posts.
- **02.4** Don't share confidential info. NDA content, private roadmap details, and internal comms stay internal.
- **02.5** Be helpful, not noisy. Answer questions when you can. Point people to docs or the right channel.
- **02.6** No recruiting, sales pitches, or unrelated promotions.

## 03 Rules for public posts (X, LinkedIn, YouTube, blogs)

### Do

- **03.1** Show real work. Walk through an actual project or result. No vague hype.
- **03.2** Include proof: screenshots, recordings, diffs, or repo links.
- **03.3** Tag @FactoryAI so the team can find and amplify it.
- **03.4** Disclose your Guild membership when relevant (for example, you received credits or perks). Transparency builds trust.
- **03.5** Be honest about your experience. Share what works and what doesn't, constructively, not as a dunk. Genuine feedback is valuable; public complaints without context aren't.
- **03.6** Read the room. Avoid tone-deaf or poorly timed posts, such as celebratory content during industry layoffs or crises.

### Don't

- **03.7** Don't misrepresent Factory. No claims about features, pricing, or partnerships you can't verify. If unsure, ask the team first.
- **03.8** Don't post ragebait or inflammatory takes. High impressions from ragebait are not a win.
- **03.9** Don't trash other tools or communities. Celebrate what works for you without belittling alternatives.
- **03.10** Don't engage with trolls. Flag inflammatory behavior to the team and move on.
- **03.11** No hateful, discriminatory, or harassing language. Zero tolerance, even as a "joke."

## 04 Submissions

Every submission logged on the Guild Contributions page should be:

- **04.1** Public and linkable
- **04.2** Showing Factory doing real work, not vague praise
- **04.3** Useful: someone else could learn from or reproduce it
- **04.4** Your original work

## 05 Enforcement

Factory reserves the right to remove members, revoke access, or decline
submissions at any time. Serious or repeated violations result in removal from
the Guild and community spaces.

## Implications for design work

- Marketing or social assets that mention Factory should show real work and
  proof, not vague hype. Prefer real screenshots over invented ones.
- Never imply Factory endorsement, partnerships, or features you cannot verify.
- Follow the logo rules in `brand-kit.md` for anything that could pass as an
  official Factory asset.
