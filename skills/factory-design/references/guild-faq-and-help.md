## Common questions

**How many Droid credits do I get?**
$200 in Droid credits every month while you stay active as an Apprentice.
Higher ranks unlock greater rewards.

**How do the credits arrive?**
As a voucher in your perks. Redeeming it adds the credits to your Factory
account balance.

**Does the voucher change my plan?**
No. Your plan stays the same. The credits wait in your balance until you use
them.

**What can credits pay for?**
A plan or extra usage. For example, if you upgrade to Max, the upgrade is paid
from your credits.

**How do I log a contribution?**
Go to Contributions, add a title, and add a link to your post, article, video,
or repository if there is one. A type and its points are suggested, and you
can change them before submitting. If the type is unclear, the Guild Council
chooses it during review.

**What if a contribution has several links?**
Add them all to one contribution, such as the repo and the posts about it. The
main link, the one worth the most, earns its full points. Each extra link
earns 1 point as a share.

**How are contributions reviewed?**
The Guild Council reviews each contribution and awards points when approved.
More effort earns more points. Each shows "In review" in your history until a
decision is made, and approved points appear in your points history on the
Contributions page.

**What if a contribution is not approved?**
You will see a note from the reviewer in your history. Use Edit to make the
changes, and it goes back to the Guild Council for review.

**How do ranks work?**
Ranks are reviewed periodically. Points are not the whole picture: quality and
how you show up for the community also count. Higher ranks unlock greater
rewards.

**What do Apprentices get?**
Guild Slack access, early feature access, $200 in Droid credits every month
while active, a Guild welcome kit, and group mentorship sessions with Factory
engineers.

**Do bug reports and feedback count?**
Yes. Share them in `#ext-guild` on Slack. Bug reports and feedback from that
channel are tracked automatically, so there is no need to log them.

**Do I need to log Guild events I attend?**
No. Attendance, volunteering, and co-hosting are recorded automatically by the
Guild Council. Use Contributions to log posts, articles, videos, and projects.

**Where will my merch ship?**
To the shipping address on your profile. Every member gets the welcome kit,
Craftsman adds the premium kit, and Artisan adds the elite kit.

**Can I use the Factory logo?**
Yes. Download the logos from the brand kit and follow the logo rules. Do not
change the colors or shape. See `brand-kit.md`.

**Where can I get help?**
Ask about ranks, points, events, and perks in `#ext-guild` on Slack, where the
Guild Council also answers membership questions. For help with Droid itself,
use the Factory Discord.
